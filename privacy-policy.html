Privacy Policy — Verra

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Verra ("we", "us", "Verra", the "App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Verra mobile application for pelvic-floor and general wellness training. By using Verra you agree to this Policy.

1. Who we are

Verra is a wellness and pelvic-floor training application. Data controller: Verra. Contact: thebukharianxo@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account & identity. Your phone number (used to create your account and to send a one-time verification code by SMS). If you choose Google Sign-In, we receive your basic Google account identifier and email.
  • Health & fitness data. Training sessions you complete, streaks, progress and strength metrics, and—if you connect it—activity and calorie data you allow us to read from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS).
  • Nutrition data & photos. Meals you log and photos of food you capture for analysis, together with the nutrition estimates produced from them.
  • Usage & device data. App interactions, diary entries, preferences (such as language), and basic technical data needed to operate and secure the service.
  • Subscription data. Your subscription status. Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we do not receive or store your full card details.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and log you in.
  • To provide training programs, track your progress, and personalize your plan.
  • To analyze food photos you submit and return nutrition estimates.
  • To operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.
  • To manage subscriptions and comply with legal obligations.

4. How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers who process it on our behalf, under contract:

  • Supabase — backend hosting, database, authentication and file storage.
  • Google — AI analysis of food photos (Gemini) and Google Sign-In / Firebase services.
  • SMS provider (Eskiz) — delivery of your one-time login codes.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — subscription billing.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

5. Health data

Data you provide about your body, training, and activity is sensitive. We use it only to deliver the App's features to you, we do not use it for advertising, and we never sell it. If you connect Health Connect or Apple Health, we access only the data types you explicitly authorize and use them solely in-app.

6. Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time from within the App or by contacting thebukharianxo@gmail.com; we will delete or anonymize it except where retention is legally required.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored on access-controlled infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children

Verra is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact thebukharianxo@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
